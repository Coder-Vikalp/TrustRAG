# BDACC Technologies — Leave Policy

**Status:** Synthetic project data
**Department:** Human Resources
**Classification:** Internal

## Leave entitlements

For this fictional policy, each employee receives the following leave
entitlements per calendar year:

- Annual leave: 18 working days.
- Sick leave: 10 working days.

These values are synthetic and are not actual BDACC Technologies
employment terms.

## Requesting annual leave

1. Submit a leave request to your reporting manager at least
   3 working days before the intended leave.
2. Include the requested dates, duration, and a brief reason.
3. The manager reviews staffing requirements and approves or rejects
   the request.
4. HR records the approved leave in the leave register.

Employees should wait for approval before taking planned annual leave.

## Reporting sick leave

Employees who cannot work due to illness should inform their manager
as soon as reasonably possible.

The manager records the absence and informs HR when required.
HR updates the leave register according to the applicable leave category.

## Responsibilities

- Employees submit accurate leave requests.
- Managers review requests and communicate decisions.
- HR maintains leave records and answers policy questions.

Human Resources maintains this synthetic policy for TrustRAG evaluation.
