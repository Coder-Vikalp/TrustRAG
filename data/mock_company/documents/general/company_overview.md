## About the company

BDACC Technologies is a fictional company created for the TrustRAG project.
Its knowledge base is used to develop and evaluate a permission-aware
enterprise knowledge assistant.

All company details, policies, budgets, and procedures used in this
corpus are synthetic and do not represent actual organizational records.

## Departments

BDACC Technologies has four primary operational departments:

- Engineering: develops, tests, and maintains software systems.
- Human Resources (HR): manages employee policies and onboarding.
- Finance: manages department budgets and expense approvals.
- Security: coordinates security procedures and incident response.

## General working practices

Employees should consult the appropriate internal documentation when
performing work.

Department-specific questions should be answered using the corresponding
official policy document.

Access to internal information depends on the user's assigned roles
and the access-control rules defined by TrustRAG.
