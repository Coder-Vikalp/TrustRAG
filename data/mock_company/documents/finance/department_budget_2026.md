## Expense approval threshold

- Expenses up to and including INR 50,000 require approval from
  the responsible department manager.
- Expenses above INR 50,000 require written approval from Finance
  before the purchase or commitment is made.
- Requests must not be split into smaller transactions to avoid
  the approval threshold.

An approval request does not guarantee that funds will be granted.

## Requesting budget approval

1. Identify the department and the relevant budget category.
2. State the amount requested and explain its business purpose.
3. Include a cost estimate and supporting documentation.
4. Obtain department manager approval.
5. Send requests above the threshold to Finance for written review.
6. Retain the approval record with the expense documentation.

Finance checks the available budget and records the final decision.
